You are a plant mage, wandering the ruins of an ancient civilisation whose towers and temples now drift as floating islands in the sky. Its people vanished long ago, but their magic still sleeps in the soil. At the end of the road, on the last island, the Heart Tree waits.

The gaps between the islands are too wide to jump. Pick up seeds (Q), plant them in the soil patches (E) and water the sprouts (F): vines grow into bridges, beanstalks into ladders, mushrooms into stepping platforms. Your watering can holds only two charges, so refill it at lakes and waterfalls, or grow a Dew plant that collects water for you.

Look around before you plant: seeds are scattered over the islands and your pockets are small, so carry only what the next gap needs. Falling is harmless, you just respawn. Take your time, learn each level, and come back to beat your best time.
