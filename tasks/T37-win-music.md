# T37 — Win-condition music

**Owner:** A7 · **Depends on:** T17, T19
**Status:** DONE

## Goal
A short, warm music sting (or stinger plus soft loop on the win panel) when the Heart Tree / goal is reached. Calm, airy, harp/pad like the rest of the score; different from the 8-bit music.

## Acceptance
- Plays on `GameEvents.GameWon` in both modes; music ducks or stops the level track first.
- Respects the Music volume and On/Off setting.
- Generated by a script in `docs/audio/` (`make_audio.py`) and logged if sourced.
- Optional: a shorter, sadder sting for `GameFailed`.

## Handoff
New `music_win.wav` (docs/audio/make_audio.py `win_music()`, ~16 s calm pad + harp, not looped) plays through `AudioManager.winMusic` on `GameWon` (crossfades the level track out, follows Music volume/toggle). The existing `win_fanfare`/`fail_sting` SFX still play on top. Field wired in Main.unity and MainSceneBuilder. Not yet listened to in play mode.
