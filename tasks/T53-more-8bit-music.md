# T53 — More 8-bit music: one tune per level

**Owner:** A7 (+ A5 for the level to track mapping) · **Depends on:** T17, T37, T43
**Status:** DONE

## Goal
Every track stays 8-bit (square / triangle / noise chip voices, generated by `docs/audio/make_audio.py` `chip(...)`, no third-party audio), but each has its own melody, key and mood, matched to the level theme.

- **Menu:** one tune (exists as `music_menu`; keep or replace, it must be distinct from every gameplay tune).
- **Level 1 and all generated levels (random, seed code):** one shared tune (the current `music_gameplay`, sunny and bouncy).
- **Each other pregenerated level gets its own tune** (levels 2-10), mood from its theme:
  2 Beanstalk Hill (Autumn), 3 Water from the Sky (Rain, quiet), 4 Skyfall (Sunny, soaring), 5 Stepping Stones (Sunny, puzzle-like), 6 There and Back (Sunrise, warm and hopeful), 7 Snowed In (Snow, sparse and icy), 8 Windy Summit (Sunny, driving, rising), 9 Into the Mist (Fog, slow and mysterious), 10 Storm Season (Rain + gusts, tense, faster).
- Win music (`music_win`) stays shared.

## Notes
- `AudioManager.GameplayTrack()` currently picks by theme only; change it to pick by `RunConfig.levelId` first (curated levels), then fall back: levelId null (random / seed code) and level 1 use the shared tune. Add a `levelMusic` list (levelId, clip) or per-level fields, built by `MainSceneBuilder` from `Audio/Music`.
- New tracks go through `chip(name, bpm, tonic, scale, progression, lead_duty, drums, seed, ...)`; vary scale (major, minor, dorian, add pentatonic / lydian if cheap), tempo, duty cycle, drums on / off and arp voicing so they are clearly different.
- Keep files small (22 kHz, mono), loops seamless, music volume from `GameSettings`. Log nothing in `LICENSES.md` (own generated audio).
- Existing tracks `music_rain` and `music_autumn` can be reused for levels 3 and 2 if they fit, but each level must still end up with a different tune from every other level.

## Acceptance
- Ten distinct gameplay tunes in total (levels 1-10) plus the menu and win tunes; random and seed-code levels play the level 1 tune.
- Starting each curated level plays its own track; switching level or returning to the menu crossfades correctly.
- No console errors; a short listening pass noted in the handoff.
