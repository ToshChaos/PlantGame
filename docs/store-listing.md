**Title:** Overgrown
**Short description (80):** Grow plants into bridges and ladders to cross floating islands.
**Full description:**
Overgrown is a calm exploration puzzle on floating islands. You can't cross the gaps — so you grow your way over them.

- Find seeds, carry up to four, plant them in soil and water them with your can.
- Vines become bridges, beanstalks become ladders, mushrooms become stairs; a Water Collector keeps your can full.
- Hand-made levels plus endless random worlds. Every world has a seed code you can copy and share with friends.
- Soft, glowing fantasy look and relaxing music. No timer pressure, no lose condition — falling just puts you back. Chase your best time if you like.
- Touch controls: tap to walk, tap markers to plant, water and pick up. Works offline, no ads, no purchases.
