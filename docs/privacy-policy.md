# Overgrown — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-29_

Overgrown is an offline single-player game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

- **Data on your device:** your settings, best times and the last seed code are saved locally on your device and removed when you uninstall the game. Apple's and Google's own store and crash reporting (if you opted in to share diagnostics with developers on your device) are separate from the game.
- **No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no network access.** The game does not need an internet connection, makes no network requests and does not request any permission (on iOS, pasting a seed code may show the system's own "Allow Paste" prompt; nothing is read unless you allow it).
