# Overgrown — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-29_

Overgrown is an offline single-player game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

- **Data on your device:** your settings, best times and the last seed code are saved locally on your device and removed when you uninstall the game.
- **No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no network access.** The game does not need an internet connection and does not request any permission.
